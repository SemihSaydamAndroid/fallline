Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Türkçe metin aşağıda.

This Privacy Policy describes how Semih Saydam ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the game Fall Line (the "App"), available on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Information We Collect

None. The App has no accounts, no online features, no ads and no analytics. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.

  • Data stored on your device. Your settings (music and sound volume, colour-blind palette, high contrast, reduced motion, slow mode, hints) are stored locally on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App.
  • Crash reports. If you install a test version through Apple TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send through TestFlight with us. On App Store versions, crash reports reach us only if you have agreed to share analytics with app developers in your device settings.

What We Don't Do

The App shows no ads, does not track you across other apps or websites, does not sell data and has no in-app purchases.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted at this URL.
