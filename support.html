How do I play?

  • Lifts go up. Pick a lift and drag uphill from beside a lodge.
  • Runs go down. Drag downhill from the top station back toward the lodge. The slope decides the colour: green, blue, red or black.
  • Match the skiers. Each lodge's skiers only take runs up to their level. Keep every lodge happy until spring.
  • Erase: hold your finger and drag, right-drag with a mouse, or hold B on a controller.
  • See the slopes: the mountain button (or V on a keyboard) tints the terrain by steepness.

Is there anything to buy?

No. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no account.
